Clips Uploader – Privacy Policy

Clips Uploader runs only on the owner's computer. It requests the TikTok scopes user.info.basic and video.publish solely to upload videos to the authorizing user's account.

Access tokens are stored locally on that computer and are never sent to any third party. No personal data is collected, sold or shared.

To delete all data, revoke the app's access in TikTok settings and delete the local token file.
